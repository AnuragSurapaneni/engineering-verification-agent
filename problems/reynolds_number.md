# Reynolds Number Calculation

## Problem Statement

Air flows through a circular pipe.

**Given Conditions:**
- Temperature: 300 K
- Pressure: 1 atm (101,325 Pa)
- Velocity: 20 m/s
- Pipe diameter: 0.1 m

**Task:** Calculate the Reynolds number and determine whether the flow is laminar or turbulent.

## Required Fluid Properties (at 300 K, 1 atm)

For air at these conditions:
- Density (ρ): ~1.177 kg/m³
- Dynamic viscosity (μ): ~1.85 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s
- Kinematic viscosity (ν): ~1.57 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s

## Governing Equation

Re = ρ × V × D / μ = V × D / ν

## Flow Regime Criteria

- Re < 2300: Laminar flow
- 2300 ≤ Re ≤ 4000: Transitional
- Re > 4000: Turbulent flow
