# Plane-Wall Conduction Heat-Rate Verification

**Problem type:** Plane-wall conduction.

A steady one-dimensional heat flow passes through a plane wall.

## Known values
- Thermal conductivity: 15 W/m/K
- Surface area: 2 m2
- Hot-side temperature: 400 K
- Cold-side temperature: 300 K
- Wall thickness: 0.05 m

## Reported result
Heat transfer rate = 60,000 W
