# Drag Force Calculator Example

## Problem Statement

A car is traveling at 30 m/s. The car has a frontal area of 2.5 m² and a drag coefficient of 0.3. The air density is 1.225 kg/m³.

**Given Conditions:**
- Fluid: Air at sea level
- Velocity (V): 30 m/s
- Frontal area (A): 2.5 m²
- Drag coefficient (C_D): 0.3
- Density (ρ): 1.225 kg/m³

**Task:** Calculate the drag force using the drag equation.

## Governing Equation

**Drag Equation:** F_D = ½ * ρ * V² * A * C_D

## Reported Result

F_D = 413.4375 N (reported by student)
