## Problem Statement

**Given Conditions:**
- Temperature: 300 K
- Pressure: 1 atm (101,325 Pa)
- Velocity: 20 m/s
- Pipe diameter: 0.1 m

**Task:** Calculate the Reynolds number and determine whether the flow is laminar or turbulent.

- Dynamic viscosity (μ): **NOT PROVIDED**
