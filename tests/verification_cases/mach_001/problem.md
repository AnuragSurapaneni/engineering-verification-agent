# Mach Number Calculation - Test Case 001

## Problem Statement

An aircraft is flying at a velocity of 250 m/s at sea level where the speed of sound is 340 m/s.

**Given Conditions:**
- Fluid: Air at sea level
- Velocity (V): 250 m/s
- Speed of sound (a): 340 m/s

**Task:** Calculate the Mach number using the Mach number equation.

## Governing Equation

**Mach Number:** M = V / a

## Reported Result

M = 0.735 (reported by student)
